## Added
- **Record Teams and Zoom calls.** "Record call" in the menu bar or the Inbox captures the call's audio and your microphone, and the recording waits in your Inbox like any other. Nothing is processed until you ask.
- **Speaker-labelled transcripts.** Recorded calls are transcribed as `You:` and `Others:` turns. Sound from your speakers leaking into the microphone is removed. Everyone on the far side of the call appears as "Others".
- **Call detection.** distill can notify you when a Zoom or Teams call starts, so you can click to record. It never records on its own. Turn it off in Settings → General.
- **The right microphone.** If the call app uses a headset you chose inside the app, distill records that one rather than the system default.
- **Updates from GitHub.** distill checks for new versions, shows you what changed before anything else, and downloads and installs only when you say so. Turn off in Settings → General, or use Check for updates in the menu bar.

## Fixed
- Your microphone no longer drops out for the rest of the call when you switch between speakers and headphones.
- A call interrupted by a crash or a forced quit is recovered into your Inbox next time distill starts.
- You are warned during a call if your microphone, or the call audio, is silent.

## Notes
- Needs macOS 14.2 or later for call recording, and ffmpeg (`brew install ffmpeg`).
- The first call recording asks for microphone and system-audio permission.
- Headphones give the cleanest transcripts.
